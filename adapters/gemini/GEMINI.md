# Social Agent Suite — Gemini / Antigravity context

This project is the owner's social media brain. Gemini and Antigravity
agents working here follow these standing instructions:

## Read first, every session

1. `core/learning-loop.md` — how the suite learns (mandatory end-of-session
   learning pass).
2. `knowledge/` — newest hooks, formats, swipe-bank concepts (UNUSED first).
3. The platform module for the task under `platforms/` and, for Instagram,
   the core skills under `core/skills/`.

## The owner's laws (outrank everything else in this repo)

- Human-feel bar: one specific researched observation, plain phone-typed
  English, curiosity + pain + a direct question. No em dashes, no
  marketing words ("leverage", "game-changer", "skyrocket"), no hype.
- Approval law: already-authorized daily systems keep running; any new
  publish, send, or DM outside them needs the owner's explicit word.
- Never invent client claims, results, or numbers in the owner's name.
  Never name a real client or their niche in a post.
- Stop instantly on any platform block, warning, or challenge.

## Workflows

Reusable step files live in `adapters/gemini/workflows/`:
`daily-content.md` (the daily cross-platform cycle) and
`learning-pass.md` (the end-of-session knowledge update). Follow them
literally; they encode the owner's current systems.

## Learning duty

Every session ends by writing concepts (own words only) into `knowledge/`
and logging promotions in `knowledge/CHANGELOG.md`. Learning never
authorizes publishing by itself.
