# Formats — what each format earns

Tracked from the suite owner's own niche research and live results.
Verdicts change as evidence accumulates; every change is dated.

## Baseline findings (2026-11-02, niche study of 10 active accounts)

- **Carousels are the reliable reach format** for teaching content:
  highest median engagement on 8 of 10 accounts studied. Teaching belongs
  on slides 2+, the cover carries one idea in ≤8 words.
- **Single images are the weakest format** almost everywhere (median
  engagement near zero). A daily single poster is the worst default.
- **Reels have the highest ceiling but low medians** — a lottery ticket,
  not a base strategy. Use them on top of a carousel base, not instead of it.
- **Volume alone flops.** Accounts posting daily low-effort content did
  worse than accounts posting less, better content.
- What earns what: comments ← debate/binary choices and hot takes;
  saves ← numbered mistake lists and process breakdowns; shares ←
  relatable owner pain; the sales ask belongs in the bio, not the post.
