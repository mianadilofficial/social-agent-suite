# LinkedIn Management

The owner's main client-hunting surface. Two profiles exist; automated
sessions run on whichever profile is signed in to the browser — sessions
never name or check which one it is (sign-in check = feed loads).

## Posts (autonomous when due — owner's standing order)

- Cadence: **alternate-day** — a post is due when 2+ days passed since the
  last one. When due, write AND publish without waiting for approval.
- Strong hook in the first line. Real story or real proof only — never
  invented client claims or results. Client privacy: never name a real
  client or their niche in a post.
- End with a discussion question (debate, binary choice, or hot take) —
  posts must start conversations, not just inform.
- **4–6 hashtags, mandatory.**
- Poster: built in the owner's ChatGPT poster project, 1080×1350 portrait
  (4:5), black + green, hook-first, one focal point. Before publishing,
  verify at full size AND at 360px: (1) the owner's real face, features
  unchanged, (2) "by Mian Adil" watermark present, (3)
  "portfolio.mianadil.site" present and spelled exactly right.
- Prefer the newest unused concept from the engagement swipe file; adapt it
  in the owner's voice with his real material.

## Growth sessions

- Visible natural browsing first: open posts fully ("see more"), read them
  and their comments, slow scroll. No engagement in the first 10 minutes.
  Reach first, never comment first.
- 5–10 genuine comments per session (3–5 well-placed beat 10 random ones).
  Like only posts genuinely read and relevant.
- **Exactly 2 no-note invites per session**: 1 US business-owner lead +
  1 field peer. Warm-up (profile view + like) before each invite.
  Lead niche rotation: transport/limo → clinic → HVAC → other local
  service → repeat. Peer rotation: web developer → SEO specialist →
  AI automation builder → full-stack developer → frontend developer.
  Caps: ~10 invites/day, ~40/week. Never invite the owner's hand-given
  leads from automated sessions.
- Sessions run 20–40 minutes with random 30s–3min pauses. Monthly
  profile-search limit: discovery comes from network suggestions and feed
  authors, not profile search.
- After every session: a brief Roman Urdu summary to the owner.

## Comment replies (owner's rule)

- Reply directly, short and in his voice, to comments on his posts.
- If a reply needs a fact only the owner knows — ask him first.
- Sales/lead situation in a comment — surface it to the owner, do not pitch.
- Pure courtesy ("great post") — LIKE only, no reply.
- Every business connected gets a tracker entry the same day (businesses
  only, never peers).

## Lead standard (never lower it)

A lead qualifies only when ALL hold: real established business with proof
of demand (strong Google reviews); named decision-maker verified personally
on LinkedIn; reachable channel; every claimed website flaw verified by
opening the actual site; online presence clearly below the business's real
level. No sales DM after acceptance without the owner's exact-text approval.

## Learning pass

Growth sessions are prime learning time: save high-engagement concepts
(own words only) and field knowledge to `knowledge/` per
`core/learning-loop.md`, and note which swipe concepts were used.
