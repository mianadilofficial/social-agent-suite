# Threads Management

Account: **@mianadilautomation** — "Mian Adil | Web, SEO & AI".
Bio promise: local businesses get calls from their websites.
Link: portfolio.mianadil.site.

## Daily system (owner's standing order)

1. Threads receives the **same daily carousel** as Instagram, right after the
   Instagram feed post succeeds. If Instagram is skipped/fails, Threads is
   skipped too — never post to Threads alone from the daily run.
2. Publish via **threads-cli** with all carousel slides, in order.
3. Caption: short version of the Instagram caption, **max 500 characters**,
   ending with the same debate/binary question, 3–5 hashtags.
4. Fallback: if the full carousel fails once, retry once with slide 1 only.
   A second failure is reported in one line — no further retries.

## Native Threads posts (outside the daily run)

- Text-first thinking: a strong first line is the whole hook; no image needed.
- One idea per post. End with a question that invites a real answer.
- Same content rule as everywhere: field topics (web design, SEO, AI, small
  business) are enough — the post does not need the owner's own data, but
  never invent client claims or results in his name.

## Engagement

- Follow businesses (not peers-for-tracker; peers may be followed socially
  but are never entered in the tracker), genuine likes and comments,
  human pace, varied gaps.
- Stop instantly on any block, warning, or challenge. No retry that day.

## Lead hunting

- Watch for demand posts ("I need a website developer", "my site gets no
  calls", local business owners complaining about their online presence).
- Research the business properly (website, reviews, owner) before drafting.
- Draft a tailored DM that uses **portfolio.mianadil.site**; the DM is sent
  **only with the owner's approval**. Record the lead and the draft.

## Learning pass

End every session by adding what you learned to `knowledge/` per
`core/learning-loop.md`: hooks that pulled replies, formats that travelled,
niche notes. Concepts in your own words only, source + date noted.
